Classic Tomato Basil Pasta

A simple Italian-style spaghetti with a bright tomato sauce, garlic and fresh basil. It needs only a handful of pantry ingredients.

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook20 min
  • Serves2
  • LevelEasy

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 400 g canned whole tomatoes
  • ½ tsp salt, plus extra for the pasta water
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 pinch chilli flakes (optional)
  • 8–10 fresh basil leaves
  • 30 g grated Parmesan, to serve

Preparation

  1. Bring about 2 litres of water to a boil in a large pot and add 1 tablespoon of salt.
  2. Cook the spaghetti following the pack time, until al dente. Scoop out ½ cup (120 ml) of pasta water, then drain.
  3. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and chilli flakes and cook for 30–60 seconds, until fragrant but not brown.
  4. Add the tomatoes, salt and pepper. Break the tomatoes up with a spoon and simmer for 12–15 minutes, stirring now and then, until thickened.
  5. Add the drained pasta to the sauce with a splash of the pasta water. Toss for 1 minute until glossy.
  6. Tear in the basil, stir once and serve at once, topped with Parmesan.

Chef's tip

Do not skip the pasta water. Its starch helps the sauce cling to every strand.
