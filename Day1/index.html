Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Biography

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes and was closely associated with the development of India's missile technology. His contributions to India's civilian space programme and military missile development earned him the title "Missile Man of India".

Beyond his scientific career, Dr. Kalam was known for his dedication to education and inspiring young people. He received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award, in 1997.

Achievements

  • Played a significant role in India's space programme.
  • Contributed to India's missile development programme.
  • Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna in 1997.
  • Received several national and international honours.
  • Authored books including Wings of Fire.
